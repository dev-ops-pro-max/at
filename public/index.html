BEFORE YOU SET OFF

A little clarity
goes a long way.

Is my booking confirmed on this website?

No. Your form is saved as an enquiry for our team. Perumal must confirm vehicle availability, the final price and booking terms directly.

How are rentals priced?

Rates depend on route, date, vehicle category, duration and distance. Ask for the complete tariff, including driver allowance, extra kilometres, tolls, parking, permits and applicable taxes.

Can I request employee transport for an IT company?

Yes. Use the corporate enquiry form to share routes, shifts, passenger numbers and your expected start date. Scope and availability are discussed before any agreement.

Can I book a specific vehicle model?

You can request one. The model, seating, luggage space and amenities must be confirmed in the final quotation.

What about cancellation or refunds?

Ask Perumal for cancellation, advance payment and refund terms before confirming or paying. No payment is taken on this website.
