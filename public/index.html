Before you set offBooking, pricing & common questions+
Is my booking confirmed on this website?

No. Your form is saved as an enquiry for our team. Perumal must confirm vehicle availability, the final price and booking terms directly.

Chennai rental prices · market guide

All prices are negotiable. Please check with Perumal for your final price, vehicle availability and applicable charges before confirming your booking.

Published operator tariffs checked on 6 November 2026. These are comparison ranges from Murugan Cabs and CMT, not Amaravathi Travels prices or a booking offer. Request your own written quote from Perumal.

Sedan / hatchback: local 5 hours / 50 km: ₹1,300–₹1,400; local 10 hours / 100 km: ₹2,600–₹2,800. Published outstation rates: ₹13–₹14 per km, minimum 250 km per calendar day, plus ₹600 driver allowance per day. Murugan groups sedan and hatchback together; CMT lists sedan separately.

Standard AC Tempo Traveller: Murugan lists 12+1 to 14+1 seats; CMT lists 12 seats. Local 5 hours / 50 km: ₹2,400; local 10 hours / 100 km: ₹4,800. Published outstation rates: ₹21–₹23 per km, minimum 250 km per calendar day, plus ₹700–₹800 driver allowance per day. Exact seating and vehicle specification must be confirmed.

Compare the total: per-km rates are subject to the daily minimum. Toll, parking, permits, tax treatment, extra hours, night charges and pickup-to-drop versus garage-to-garage billing can differ. Confirm all inclusions with the operator. CMT states GST-inclusive prices; Murugan lists taxes as additional for outstation travel. One-way and round-trip quotations are not directly interchangeable.

Sources: Murugan Cabs published tariff · CMT published tariff. Rates may change after the check date.

How are rentals priced?

Rates depend on route, date, vehicle category, duration and distance. Ask for the complete tariff, including driver allowance, extra kilometres, tolls, parking, permits and applicable taxes.

Can I request employee transport for an IT company?

Yes. Use the corporate enquiry form to share routes, shifts, passenger numbers and your expected start date. Scope and availability are discussed before any agreement.

How much luggage can we bring?

Tell us the passenger count, number of suitcases and any large items when requesting your quote. Ask Perumal to confirm the luggage space for your assigned vehicle before booking. Seating and luggage capacity depend on the vehicle provided.

What should I confirm before paying an advance?

Request a written quote with the vehicle category, pickup time, itinerary, included hours and kilometres, extra charges and total payable. Confirm the advance amount, payment details, cancellation charges and refund terms directly with Perumal before paying. This website does not collect payments or confirm a booking automatically.

Can I book a specific vehicle model?

You can request one. The model, seating, luggage space and amenities must be confirmed in the final quotation.

What about cancellation or refunds?

Ask Perumal for cancellation, advance payment and refund terms before confirming or paying. No payment is taken on this website.
