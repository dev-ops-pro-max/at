Website privacy notice

Last updated: 3 November 2026. This notice covers the Amaravathi Travels rental enquiry website, not the Android app.

Information you submit

We collect your name, phone number, trip or corporate requirements, and the time of your enquiry when you submit a form. Driver partner enquiries may include vehicle type, operating area and experience. Please do not submit identity documents, passwords, or payment card details.

How information is used

We use your enquiry to respond, prepare quotations, arrange requested services and maintain booking records. Approved staff can view and update these records.

Service providers

Cloudflare hosts this website and database, protects forms with Turnstile, and authenticates staff through Access. Technical connection information may be processed for security and operating the service. If you choose WhatsApp, your message is handled by WhatsApp under its own terms and privacy policy. You must press Send in WhatsApp yourself.

Retention and deletion

Enquiry records are automatically removed 180 days after their last update. If a booking needs longer-term records for accounting or other obligations, the team must explain that separately when arranging your service. To request access, correction or deletion, email amaravathitravelsavr@gmail.com with your enquiry reference. We may need to verify that the request relates to you.

Security and cookies

Staff access is restricted and traffic uses HTTPS. Cloudflare security and authentication features may use necessary cookies or browser storage. This release does not add advertising trackers. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Contact

Amaravathi Travels, Chennai, Tamil Nadu. Email amaravathitravelsavr@gmail.com or call +91 99402 63719.
